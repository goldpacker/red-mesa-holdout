Hold the mesa. Survive ten waves.

You command a three-weapon gun emplacement on a red-rock mesa. An enemy army is parachuting into the canyon below: infantry, buggies, tanks, helicopters, jets, and finally the Siege Crawler, a colossal land fortress.

• Machine gun (watch the heat), rockets for armour, AA missiles for aircraft
• Enemies airdrop anywhere across the basin, so you never know where the next stick lands
• 10 escalating waves, from bright afternoon to a golden sunset finale
• Dust storms, supply crate drops, combo scoring and Retry Wave checkpoints
• Boss fight: shoot off the Crawler's turrets and cannon, then destroy its core

Controls: mouse to aim · left click to fire · hold right click for the gunsight · 1 / 2 / 3 or the wheel to switch weapons

Single player. Best on PC with a mouse.
