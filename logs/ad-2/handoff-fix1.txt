DropLoadId; a sighting is kept until the load is done (no ChildRemoved clearing, so an add +
remove between two frames still counts); with nothing seen by landT + AirdropStyle.HANDOFF_GRACE
(0.4 s) the body is retired anyway (counter AirdropFx.HandoffsRetired). The canopy collapses
at touchdown as always; only the body hides.

DropLoadIds, on the tick the enemy spawns does one of:

  delete = model:Destroy()
